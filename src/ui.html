Script

Paste your sermon or lesson. Lines starting with # are section titles. Cues in brackets show as signals and are never waited for: [pause] [build] [softer] [slide] [verse: John 3:16] [ad-lib: story].

Slides

In PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides, save or download your slides as a PDF, then load it here. Put [slide] in your script wherever the next slide should appear, and it changes by itself when you get there. A clicker or the arrow keys work too.

Audience screen opens your slides in their own window with smooth transitions. Drag it onto the projector or TV and click it for full screen. In Zoom or Teams, share that window only, and your prompter stays private.

Settings

Automatic uses your browser's own speech recognition when you're online, and the on-device engine when you're offline or the browser can't listen. The on-device engine downloads once (about 40 MB), then works without internet. Speech language applies to browser speech only.

Shortcuts: Space starts or stops your voice. Up and down arrows move a line. Tap any word to jump there.
